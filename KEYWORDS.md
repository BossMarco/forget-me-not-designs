# Forget Me Not Flowers — Target Keyword List

Domain: https://www.forgetmenotrgv.com — launched 2026-06-07.
Full machine list (600 tracked in SerpBear): `Projects/seo-stack/forgetmenot-keywords.tsv`

**Reality check:** brand-new domain. Indexing takes days–weeks; ranking for
competitive local terms ("flower shop mcallen") takes 2–6+ months and depends
heavily on a claimed/optimized **Google Business Profile** (the map pack ranks
above organic for these searches). Check progress with `site:forgetmenotrgv.com`
in Google and in Search Console → Pages.

`{city}` = each RGV city we serve (McAllen, Edinburg, Pharr, Mission, Weslaco,
Harlingen, Brownsville, South Padre Island, San Juan, Alamo, Donna, Mercedes,
Hidalgo, Palmview, Alton, Rio Grande City, Los Fresnos, San Benito, Port Isabel,
Raymondville, + ~28 smaller towns).

## How each keyword maps to a page
- `florist/flower shop/flower delivery {city}` → city hub `/flower-delivery/{city}`
- `{occasion} flowers {city}` → matrix page `/flower-delivery/{city}/{occasion}`
- `{occasion} flowers rgv` → occasion hub `/occasions/{occasion}`
- Brand terms → homepage `/`

## Priority order for ranking effort
1. Claim + optimize Google Business Profile (unlocks the map pack — biggest lever).
2. Brand terms (easiest wins; should rank in days–weeks once indexed).
3. Tier-1 city core terms (McAllen/Pharr/Edinburg/etc. florist + flower delivery).
4. Everyday occasions in tier-1 cities (birthday/anniversary/funeral).
5. Long-tail city × occasion (lower competition, steady traffic).
